Piper Lessac
What if you woke up 100 years in the future?
You open your eyes, and the world looks completely different.
Your phone? Ancient history.
Cars might drive themselves, and artificial intelligence could be everywhere.
Cities might have buildings hundreds of floors tall, while robots handle jobs we do today.
But here's the terrifying part.
Everyone you knew would be 100 years older…
or no longer around.
Your money might be worthless.
Your home could belong to someone else.
And the world you remember would exist only in history books.
But not everything would be unfamiliar.
People would still laugh, argue, fall in love, and make friends.
Technology might change everything.
But humans?
Maybe not as much as you’d think.
Would you want to wake up in 2126?
