What if Earth suddenly became twice as big?
Not just wider. Its radius doubles, while its density stays the same.
That means Earth would have eight times its original mass.
And gravity? It would become roughly twice as strong at the surface.
Walking suddenly becomes harder. Jumping becomes nearly impossible for ordinary people to do like before. Buildings, bridges and infrastructure would face enormous stress, depending on how the transformation happened.
The atmosphere would also be affected by the planet's stronger gravity.
Oceans, mountains and the entire landscape would be transformed.
And here's the crazy part.
Earth wouldn't just be a bigger planet. It would be a completely different world.
But could humanity adapt to living on it?
